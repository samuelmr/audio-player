# Demo music

The web app at <https://audioplayer.ctrldash.app/> reads a public demo bucket
until you enter settings of your own (see *A public bucket* and *Settings* in
the [README](README.md)). This file records where the music in that bucket
comes from and under what terms, so that anyone can check that we may
redistribute it.

All of it is in the public domain: the artists and Musopen dedicated it to
the public domain with [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
CC0 waives all copyright, so the files may be copied, changed and
redistributed, also commercially, without asking or giving credit. We credit
the artists anyway, here and in the tags of the files.

The sources were checked on 6 October 2026.

### The licence on Bandcamp

Both albums are also on the artist's Bandcamp,
<https://chezmonplaisir.bandcamp.com/album/tale-on-the-late> and
<https://chezmonplaisir.bandcamp.com/album/lofi-ambient-songs>, where they are
marked with Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY 3.0 in earlier copies of the
pages, CC BY 4.0 now). As far as we know, Bandcamp offers only the Creative
Commons licences, not CC0, so artists who choose CC0 elsewhere end up with
CC BY there.

This doesn't limit our use:

- A CC0 dedication can't be withdrawn, and the artist's website and the
  Free Music Archive give CC0 for these albums.
- Even under CC BY, redistribution is allowed with credit to the artist, a
  link to the licence and a note of changes. This file and the tags of the
  files give that credit.

The Bandcamp pages also ask, without making it a condition of the licence,
that the music isn't used to oppress anyone and isn't registered with
Content ID. The demo does neither.

## Frédéric Chopin – Musopen's *Complete Chopin Collection*

- **Downloaded from:** the Internet Archive, <https://archive.org/details/musopen-chopin>.
  It was uploaded by Musopen's founder, Aaron Dunn, with the licence CC0 1.0.
- **The compositions:** Chopin died in 1849, so his works are in the public
  domain.
- **The recordings:** Musopen is a non-profit that makes recordings of
  classical music and puts them in the public domain
  (<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Musopen>). In 2013 it raised the money to
  record Chopin's works with the Kickstarter campaign *Set Chopin Free*
  (<https://www.openculture.com/2013/09/set-chopin-free.html>); this
  collection is, as far as we know, its result. The licence of the
  recordings rests on Musopen's own CC0 dedication above.

The files don't name the performers, so the bucket doesn't either. They have
no tags or cover; the player names them from their paths. The bucket has 12
of the collection's pieces, with their original file names:

- `Ballade no. 1 - Op. 23.mp3`
- `Fantasie Impromptu Op. 66.mp3`
- `Grande Valse Brilliante Op.18 In E flat major.mp3`
- `Impromptu no. 1 - Op. 29.mp3`
- `Mazurka Op. 17 no. 4 in A minor.mp3`
- `Nocturne Op. 9 no. 2 in E flat major.mp3`
- `Nocturne Op. 27 no. 1 in C sharp minor.mp3`
- `Nocturne Op. 48 no. 1 in C minor.mp3`
- `Prelude Op. 28 no. 15.mp3`
- `Waltz Op. 64 no. 1 in D flat major.mp3`

## What we changed

- **The audio** is as downloaded, not re-encoded or edited.
- **File names:** the tracks of Komiku and Loyalty Freak Music are renamed
  `NN Title.mp3` after their track numbers and titles, so that the bucket is
  organized the way the README describes. In track 10 of *Tale on the Late*
  we dropped the ` ?` from the end of the title, as `?` is awkward in web
  addresses. The tags inside the files are unchanged.
- **Covers:** each `cover.jpg` is the album art embedded in that album's MP3
  files, extracted as it is.
- **Metadata:** the S3 metadata of the tracks comes from their tags, by
  [`scripts/upload.sh`](scripts/upload.sh).
- **`Evening mix.json`** is a playlist we made of ten of these tracks. It
  holds only the tracks' keys in the bucket and their tags. Like this
  repository, it is released under the [Unlicense](LICENSE).

## Adding music

Before adding music to the demo bucket, check that its licence allows
redistribution, preferably from the artist's own pages, and add it to this
file.
